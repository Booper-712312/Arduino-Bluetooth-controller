====================================================
           PC BLUETOOTH CONTROLLER v2.0
              MAPA DE COMANDOS
====================================================

=========================
MOVIMIENTO
=========================

Tecla PC          Comando Bluetooth

W / w      ----->    F     (Forward)
S / s      ----->    B     (Back)
A / a      ----->    L     (Left)
D / d      ----->    R     (Right)

Q / q      ----->    G     (Forward Left)
E / e      ----->    I     (Forward Right)

Z / z      ----->    H     (Back Left)
C / c      ----->    J     (Back Right)

X / x      ----->    S     (Stop)

=========================
ILUMINACIÓN
=========================

I / i

Primer toque  ----->  W     (Front Lights ON)
Segundo toque ----->  w     (Front Lights OFF)

--------------------------------------------

K / k

Primer toque  ----->  U     (Back Lights ON)
Segundo toque ----->  u     (Back Lights OFF)

=========================
CLAXON
=========================

Mantener presionada H / h

Presionar -----> V    (Horn ON)

Soltar -------> v    (Horn OFF)

=========================
EXTRA
=========================

P / p

Primer toque  ----->  X     (Extra ON)

Segundo toque ----->  x     (Extra OFF)

=========================
VELOCIDADES
=========================

Tecla PC          Comando Bluetooth

|           ----->    0      (0%)

1           ----->    1      (10%)

2           ----->    2      (20%)

3           ----->    3      (30%)

4           ----->    4      (40%)

5           ----->    5      (50%)

6           ----->    6      (60%)

7           ----->    7      (70%)

8           ----->    8      (80%)

9           ----->    9      (90%)

0           ----->    q      (100%)

=========================
PROGRAMA
=========================

ESC         ----->    Cerrar el controlador
